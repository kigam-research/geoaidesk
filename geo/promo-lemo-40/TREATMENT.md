# Treatment: GAID 40 s promo (lemo-opuscar, Dark Tech Keynote)

Task `gaid-promo-video-v4-lemo--promo-40s`. Style: lemo-opuscar `styles/dark-keynote/STYLE.md` (asset
`~/assets/lemo-opuscar/3961ddb/`). 42.0 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps, stereo music + sound effects, no narration, no subtitle track:
the on-screen copy carries the message. Everything is drawn in code; real GAID takes appear only as windows inside the
motion graphics (user, 2026-10-24: "촬영 에셋이 영상 전 영역을 차지하지 않고, 해당 기능을 소개하는 모션그래픽속의 창요소처럼"),
played whole at one scale, never zoomed or scrolled inside.

## 1. Brief as understood

- Subject: GAID, "지구과학 연구자를 위한 AI agent 연구 지원 플랫폼", in two parts: GAID 허브 (Workflows made from papers, shared and
  improved, "version") and GAID 시스템 (내 서재 → 연구 분석 → 연구노트 → 내 서재).
- Messages equal to v4, direction free and promotional; 40 s means: background in two beats, the turn, the cycle as the spine,
  the ecosystem in one line, the end card.
- Rules: names "AI agent"/"GAID"; no invented numbers; no KIGAM logo; brand blue the one accent; Pretendard only.

## 2. Three candidate structures

1. **Disorder → one press → the grid → the cycle → many cycles.** The style's own arc; it is what the parent's 103 s cut does.
   Rejected as the main shape here: a second film with the same arc adds nothing.
2. **A countdown checklist** of research steps. Reads as a feature list, which the user rejected for v3.
3. **One orbit (chosen).** The protagonist is GAID's smallest living element, the orbiting dot of the AI agent (`.gaid-orbit`,
   the orbit ring of the logo). The film is one continuous orbit: a white caret lost among scattered work, a press that turns
   it blue, a ring it draws and travels (the research cycle, each station opening a real GAID window), an orbit that multiplies
   into many researchers' rings, and a ring that tilts into the orbit of the GAID mark. The background becomes the hook, the
   cycle the spine, the logo the payoff. The disorder → press beat survives as the turn because the style needs one decisive
   action, but it lasts 5 s, not a chapter.

## 3. Logline and arc

A white dot loses itself among papers, data, code and scattered tools until one press turns it blue; it draws the research
cycle and runs it through GAID, then the cycle multiplies into an institute's second brain and closes into the GAID mark.

- Setup (0–10 s): too much work, tools out of reach.
- Turn (10–15 s): freeze, silence, press; everything lands in one window.
- Development (15–30 s): the ring; six stations, each a real GAID window.
- Ending (30–42 s): many rings, KIGAM Second brain; the ring becomes the logo's orbit.

## 4. Benchmark (grammar only)

- Product keynote openers on a black stage (one line per screen, light as the reveal). Learn: negative space, one focal
  element, the accent only on the product. Do not take: any layout, device frame, typeface, melody or logo.
- Orbit/planetarium title sequences (a small body tracing a path that becomes a system). Learn: a path drawn by a moving point
  as the transition; scale reveal by pull-back. Do not take: any shot or design.
- Steve Reich, additive process. Learn: one note added per station, beat division as acceleration. Do not take: melodies.
